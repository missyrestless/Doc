Thank you for your purchase of the new Steelhead Scuba Scooter.  

You will have hours of fun with this as it provides a very relaxing auto drive function that will carry you through the waters of SL with ease.

The scooter is easy to use and easy to customize.  We even include alternative textures for you to use as well as a UV and AO map for those graphically inclined who want to make their own custom paint.

To Operate:

Rez the craft on the water surface just like you would a boat and much like a boat, these don't drive well out of the water so make sure you are in the water not the shore. DO NOT WEAR OR ATTACH THIS TO YOUR AVATAR.  Right click and select Ride. 
There are three speeds for forward and reverse.  Use you up and down arrows to change speeds.  The indicator will tell you in local chat which speed you are in.

To move up and down in the water, use the Page Up and Page down keys.

To change poses, click the sled and use the pop down window to adjust or change.

To control the lights, click the light box and select what you want from the menu.

The camera is for cosmetics only but you can change the image with a little editing skills.  There is a plastic housing over the camera you will need to move before you can access the image screen. It take a normal scaled image so you only have to drag and drop to change.  No graphics skills are needed.

And that's it! Easy to use and easy to drive.  Have fun and we hope to see you in the water.

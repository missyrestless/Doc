WANDERER SETUP HELPER


138.2,197.7,5.4,188.8,216.6,19.7

About this object

This cube tells you the necessary coordinates when you setup Dugong Janus's WANDERER marine animals.
Available up to 64-meter cube area.
Linden's official viewer 3.0 or later strongly recommended.
Older,  or third-party viewers may not allow you to stretch the object over 10 meters.

Hot To Use

1. REZ Setup Helper at the area you like to release WANDERER animals.

2. Move and stretch the object to fit the area. DO NOT ROTATE.

3. Touch the object and it tells you the coordinates.

4. Copy the coordinates (and no other text!).

5. Rez and touch WANDERER animals, and paste the coordinates to your chat window, and say it.

6. If your land is larger than you can stretch the object;
6-1. Rez 2 Setup Helpers inworld (you can duplicate it.)
6-2. place one at South-West BOTTOM in the swimming area, and the other, at North-East SURFACE.
6-3. Touch the first one and copy the first 3 figures.
6-4. Touch the other and copy the last 3 figures.
6-5. Combine these figures, and now you have got the necessary coordinates made of 6 figures.

7. After you setup the animals, you can delete the setup helper.

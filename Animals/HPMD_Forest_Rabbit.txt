About HPMD* Rabbit in Forest

􀀁

- This product includes both of animesh versions and no-animesh/static versions.
- Usually animesh objects cannot be used left-click actions like opening dialog, but Rabbit in Forest can open the dialog from right-click instead. To apply options to a specific rabbit, right-click the rabbit and select "Touch" and open a dialog. Also, when applying options to more than two rabbits at once, use a HUD that corresponds to each type of rabbit (Land, Shoulder, Static).
- As of March 2020, animish cannot be resized.
- As of March 2020, free accounts can wear one animesh object at a time, and premium accounts can wear two animesh object at a time. These limits are a restriction by Linden Lab.

 About HUD and Dialog Options
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Area of ​​effect (HUD option)
Select the range that the HUD settings are applied. After selecting a range, all options will be applied to all rabbits within that radius around the avatar.

----------------------------
 Fur alpha mode (Blending / Masking)
----------------------------
You can choose how the fur's alpha textures are displayed. Blending has a soft atmosphere but the drawing load is higher, and Masking looks hard atmosphere but the drawing load is low. Masking may look better in an environment where a lot of alpha textures are used.

----------------------------
 Delete script (Static rabbit only)
----------------------------
This is an option only for the Static version. Delete the scripts in the rabbits. This option is not applied to the HUD scripts and the animesh rabbits.

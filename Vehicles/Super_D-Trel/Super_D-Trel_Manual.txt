   8 ******* EXTERNAL POWER AND FUEL PUMP     **********
          
  The plane has a power system. When stopping and turning off the engine, be sure to turn off the MASTER, or you run out of energy after a while. You can see the battery charge on the middle down panel. If it completely discharges, you have to use EXTERNAL power. 
 MUST Brakes/ON and  MASTER/ON. So type in chat  "gpu" .  Then GPU shows out side de plane.
 There is also a FUEL system that you may Refuel. You can see  the FUEL charge on the middle down panel and turn  it OFF if  you are far from land. If not  JUST REFUEL.  (Manual Fuel Pump)

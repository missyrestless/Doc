Manual of How to Use (follow this sequence):

1 - Move your plane from the fuel pump (in front position) 
(an alarm will be played and the button ' > ' will ascend and be green) 

2 - Click the 'power' to turn on the fuel pump 
(the engines will be connected and the button 'power' will be green) 

3 - Choose the amount of fuel that will fuel your plane 
(choice number 2 in the panel number, ex: 6 and 4 ->  ' 64 ' in total) 

4 - Click on the button 'send' to the pump start sending fuel 
(panel 'sending' indicated the amount already sent) 

5 - Wait for the end of the process

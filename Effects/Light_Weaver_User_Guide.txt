LIGHT WEAVER — DRUID EDITION
User Guide

IMPORTANT — WEAR ONLY
This item must be worn as an attachment. Do not rez it on the ground; it is not designed for rezzed use.

GETTING STARTED
Find the Light Weaver in your inventory and choose Add to wear it. Touch the orb to open the owner-only menu, then select Shapes and choose a design.

10 DRAWING SHAPES
• Triquetra
• Triskele
• Oak Leaf
• Awen
• Tree of Life
• Crescent
• Sun Wheel
• Spiral
• Seed of Life
• Acorn

COLORS
Choose Pink, Gold, Blue, Purple, Green or White.

SIZE & SPEED
Choose Small, Medium, Large or Huge.
Choose Slow, Normal or Fast drawing speed.

DRAWING PLANE
• Front — draws in front of your avatar.
• Side — draws in the side plane.
• Flat — draws horizontally.

POSITION
Use Adjust to move the drawing position:
Left, Right, Up, Down, Forward or Backwards.

Save Pos saves your preferred position.
Reset Pos returns to the script’s starting position.

SOUNDS & MOVEMENT
The orb uses charge, drawing and finish sounds. Between drawings, it moves in a gentle idle orbit and returns smoothly after completing a shape.

LIGHT TRAILS
Particles gradually fade and last approximately six seconds. Longer shapes may begin fading before the drawing is complete, especially at Slow speed.

STOP & RESET
Stop stops emission when the script handles the command. During an active drawing, commands wait until that drawing finishes. Existing particles fade naturally.

Reset restarts the script and restores its default color, size, speed and drawing plane. Your saved position is retained.

TIPS
• If you cannot see the effect, check that particles are enabled in your viewer.
• Use Plane and Adjust to position the design comfortably around your avatar.
• Wait for one drawing to finish before selecting the next.
• Keep the item’s scripts, texture and sounds inside the attachment.

Enjoy weaving a little woodland magic.

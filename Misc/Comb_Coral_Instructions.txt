FAQ:
 
 -What is Blinn-Phong? - 
 -Blinn Phong, named after the last names of its inventors,  is the traditional old school SL materials, without going into the technical details, these have certain advantages, but they need to be tweaked, and are not particularly good at creating reflective materials like metals or crystals. In addition, SL Blinn-Phongs emissive can only be the same color as the color map and they are one sided, which means you need to double the geometry for things like leaves, which results in a higher LI, and you cant have both alpha and emissive without again doubling the geometry.
  
  -What is PBR?-
    Physical Based Rendering is the current standard of many 3D rendering engines, and has several advantages over the old Blinn-Phong. Its standardized, so materials that look one way in one engine will look the same on another engine using PBR, in addition the Emissive is its own RGB map, which means glow can have different colors than the color surface they are on, metallic reflections are much better and accurate and the alpha is independent of the Emissive. Also, there can be two sided materials. All this results in much more versatility and accuracy of textures, and can mean a huge reduction in geometry. For example, under the old system, to make an alpha leaf that glowed you needed one plane for the front, one plane for the back, one plane for the front glow and one plane for the back glow, even if all of them were using the same textures. Under PBR it will just be one single plane. PBR will enhance how SL looks significantly, once the bugs and current problems are ironed out. But, as I want to give you the best, I am already implemented, amongst the first designers in Second Life, full customizable scripted sets that offer both full Blinn-Phong and PBR textures, offering you the best of both worlds according to your current viewer. You will see what your viewer is capable of displaying. In the future, when PBR is more popular, it will eventually replace the old materials render system, but I will continue to at least offer a texture backup for the old system for when older viewers are used.

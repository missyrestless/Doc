Hello and thank you for your interest in this little and helpful device

this will allow you to quickly change heel shape of our Maitreya Body.

setup is not too hard.
Just activate all the five gestures provided in the "Gestures" folder.

Then copy/cut and paste the folder named "Maitreya Feet GestureSystem" from the #rlv folder into your #RLV folder.

Now wear the "Maitreya Gesture Heel Angle Set - Relay HUD"
alternatively if you don't want to see anything and don't want to click on th HUD, wear the "Maitreya Gesture Heel Angle Set - Relay Only". It will allow only gestures use.

you are ready to go!

How to change heel shapes:
method 1:
You can click the HUD to select the five available heel shapes

method 2:
you can type:
/maflat for flat shape
/malow for low shape
/mamid for mid shape
/mahigh for high shape
/mabal for ballet shape

method 3:
you can press:
CTRL F2 for flat shape
CTRL F3 for low shape
CTRL F4 for mid shape
CTRL F5 for high shape
CTRL F6 for ballet shape

Note: the system is lag free and it has only very low impact script. So no worries.

Alternatively you can copy and paste the desired item from their respective heel angle folders, and put a copy (or link, better) in your outfit folders, so you don't need neither the HUD, neither the RLV, nor the gestures!

======= If you don't know what RLV is ==========

RLV is a system that allows scripts to communicate with the viewer.
Thee viewer must include this function, so the default LL client won't work.
You can use any other RLV enabled viewer, such as Firestorm.

the #RLV folder is not a default folder, so you can create it in your inventory and be sure to name it upper case.
You will see the folder's icon to change, so it means everything is good.

Now simply put the "Maitreya Feet GestureSystem" into that folder
now go into the preferences of your Viewer and enable the Restrained Love system (Remote Scripted Viewer Controls) or also RLVa.

You will be asked to log out and log back in. 
You're ready to go
